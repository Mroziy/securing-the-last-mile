# Securing the Last Mile: Cybersecurity Education and Digital Rights for Informal Economy Fintech Users

## 📌 Project Overview
"Securing the Last Mile" is an open-source, data-driven cybersecurity curriculum and digital rights framework engineered specifically for informal economy fintech users in West Africa. As financial ecosystems rapidly transition away from legacy banking toward decentralized digital touchpoints—such as mobile money wallets, agency banking networks, and USSD protocols—vulnerable populations face escalating risks from social engineering, routing manipulation, and transaction fraud.

This project bridges the gap between technical cryptographic security and grassroots educational literacy. By translating complex cybersecurity defenses into actionable protocols, we empower everyday economic actors to protect their financial assets and assert their digital rights.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Objectives
* **Democratize Cybersecurity Literacy:** Build localized, open-access training modules that address specific vectors used against informal fintech users (e.g., USSD swapping, SMS phishing).
* **Champion Digital Rights:** Establish frameworks that educate underbanked demographics on data privacy, transaction transparency, and consumer recourse mechanisms.
* **Integrate Secure Routing Standards:** Evaluate how open financial protocols, such as the Interledger Protocol (ILP), can be securely layered with edge-level telemetry to protect the financial integrity of micro-transactions.

---

## 📖 Curriculum Architecture

### 📁 Module 1: Foundations of Digital Sovereignty & Fintech
* **Focus:** Introduction to financial data rights, understanding mobile wallet architectures, and mapping personal data footprints in the informal economy.
* **Deliverables:** Conceptual frameworks, localized privacy checklists, and data-rights mapping tools.

### 📁 Module 2: Threat Modeling for Sub-National Payment Rails
* **Focus:** Deconstructing attack vectors targeting USSD strings, SMS communication channels, and human-in-the-middle exploits at agency banking kiosks.
* **Deliverables:** Analytical threat matrices, real-world case studies, and localized defense protocols.

### 📁 Module 3: Interledger Protocol (ILP) & Cryptographic Trust
* **Focus:** Exploring the fundamentals of open-standard payment routing, verifying transaction ledger integrity, and understanding how decentralized networks mitigate centralized fraud lock-in.
* **Deliverables:** Technical architectural overviews and lightweight verification models.
